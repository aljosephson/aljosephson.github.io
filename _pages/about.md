I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Agricultural and Resource Economics and Co-Director of the [AIDE Lab](https://aidelab.arizona.edu/) at the University of Arizona. I am on sabbatical at Stellenbosch University during the 2026 - 2027 academic year.

My research focuses on the measurement and how data, methods, and assumptions shape our understanding of well-being.
