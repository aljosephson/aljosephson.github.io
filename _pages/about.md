I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Agricultural and Resource Economics and Co-Director of the [AIDE Lab](https://aidelab.arizona.edu/) at the University of Arizona.

My research focuses on measurement — how data, methods, and assumptions shape our understanding of well-being. I primarily explore these themes within the contexts of food security, agriculture, and development in Sub-Saharan Africa and Asia.

I study how households in developing countries manage food security, adopt agricultural technologies, and respond to shocks. I also work extensively on the measurement of weather and climate impacts using Earth observation data and remote sensing, investigating the consequences of measurement error for applied economic research.

Beyond empirical development economics, I am deeply interested in the process and ethics of economic research itself — focusing on informed consent, transparency, reproducibility, and the role of generative AI in applied economics.
