Anna Josephson

Associate Professor · University of Arizona

I'm an Associate Professor in the Department of Agricultural and Resource Economics and Co-Director of the AIDE Lab at the University of Arizona. My research focuses on measurement — how data, methods, and assumptions shape our understanding of well-being — with a particular focus on food security, agriculture, and development in Sub-Saharan Africa and Asia.

Research Areas

🌾 Agriculture & Food Security

How households in developing countries manage food security, adopt agricultural technologies, and respond to shocks — with field work in Ethiopia, Malawi, Zimbabwe, Bangladesh, and the Philippines.

🌍 Climate, Weather & Risk

Measurement of weather and climate impacts using Earth observation data and remote sensing, including the consequences of measurement error for applied economic research.

⚖️ Research Ethics & Methods

The process and ethics of economic research itself — informed consent, transparency, reproducibility, and the role of generative AI in applied economics.
